Steve Jobs

1955 - 2011

About Steve Jobs

Steve Jobs was an American businessman and technology entrepreneur. He was born in 1955 and died in 2011. He was one of the founders of Apple and helped develop many important technology products. His work had a major influence on personal computers and mobile devices.

Important Events

  1. 1955 - Steve Jobs was born.
  2. 1976 - He co-founded Apple.
  3. 1984 - Apple introduced the Macintosh.
  4. 1985 - He left Apple.
  5. 1997 - He returned to Apple.
  6. 2007 - Apple introduced the iPhone.

Key Contributions

  • Apple computers
  • Macintosh
  • iPod
  • iPhone
  • iPad
